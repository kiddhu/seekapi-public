# Build a supplier-ready brief

Start with the smallest complete request: product or model, quantity with unit, destination country and essential match requirements. The free form or current MCP discovery schema handles the exact accepted fields.

Ask for facts once and retain the answers. Do not turn the checklist into mandatory questions when the buyer has already supplied them.

| Buying decision | Details that affect a comparable quote | Mismatch to resolve with the buyer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fasteners or metal parts | Dimensions/thread and tolerance; material/grade; coating or finish; quantity and unit | A similar size is not an exact match; coating, grade and pack quantity can change price |
| Packaging | Internal dimensions; material/construction; print/artwork and colors; quantity per size | A stock unprinted box is not a quote for a custom printed design |
| Connectors or electrical parts | Pitch/contact count; mating interface; voltage/current requirements; environmental rating | A physically similar connector may have a different pinout or incompatible mating part |
| General product | Required function; key dimensions/materials; variant; quantity; destination | A photo or marketplace title may omit the exact variant or specification |

Use these as prompts for the buyer, not claims about supplier availability. When the buyer has not specified a fact, record it as a question or let prepare return the missing field.

## Optional commercial targets

A target unit price, sample quantity, preferred Incoterm or desired arrival date helps scope the request when supplied by the buyer. Keep targets separate from confirmed supplier offers. The USD19.90 RFQ fee is a service fee, separate from goods, samples, freight and supplier payments.

## What the buyer reviews for free

- Product, variant and must-match specifications.
- Quantity and unit.
- Destination and any stated commercial targets.
- References the buyer has authorized SeekAPI to receive.
- Any remaining questions or proposed substitutions.

Review and confirmation happen before the buyer chooses payment in the official form. USD2.99 screening is optional.

## What makes later quotes comparable

| Field | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Product and specification revision | Ties the supplier answer to the confirmed requirement |
| Quantity/unit and MOQ | Separates a listing or sample price from the requested production quantity |
| Currency and unit-price basis | Prevents comparing a carton with a single piece |
| Incoterm and destination | Separates goods price from freight or import costs |
| Lead time and quote validity | Distinguishes a current offer from an old listing |
| Supplier source and confirmation evidence | Shows which terms the supplier actually confirmed |
| Open questions and exclusions | Preserves what still needs buyer or supplier confirmation |

The human report, Agent JSON and evidence list should describe the same result.

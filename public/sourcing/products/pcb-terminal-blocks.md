# Which PCB terminal-block fields must match before you compare offers?

Match the terminal block to the board interface before comparing offers. Record pitch, position count, pin layout, mounting process and connection direction, then retain electrical ratings with their stated conditions. A familiar housing or a nearby pitch is not evidence of a drop-in replacement. Use the maker drawing for the named reference and obtain engineering confirmation for any substitute; sourcing evidence alone cannot approve an electrical design.

Materially updated: 2026-10-16

## When to use this checklist

Use this when a PCB terminal block must fit a specific board interface. Compare its drawing, pitch, pin layout, wire entry, mounting process and condition-scoped ratings before an engineer considers a substitute.

Editorial decision aid: this does not obtain quotations, contact suppliers or approve product conformity.

## Minimum buyer brief

Required reference and drawing; board interface; poles/rows; mounting; rating conditions; quantity unit.

| Field | Buyer input | Evidence check | Reject or unknown |
|---|---|---|---|
| pitch | Record the exact required spacing and drawing reference. | Compare the maker drawing with the board interface. | Nearby5.0/5.08mm labels do not prove interchangeability. |
| positions and rows | Specify number of positions, rows and connections. | Do not count a product photo as a particular configured variant. | Wrong pole count is a hard mismatch. |
| pin layout and dimensions | Record pin arrangement, pin dimensions and required board geometry. | Use the dimensional drawing for the exact part. | Same external housing does not establish same pinning. |
| mounting process | State the buyer-required mounting/solder process. | Retain the product-specific process description. | Do not assume all terminal blocks support the same process. |
| connection direction | State wire entry orientation and mechanical constraints. | Check the reference-specific geometry. | An obstructed or reversed interface is not a permitted substitute. |
| condition-scoped ratings | Record rating plus the conditions and documentation named by the maker. | Preserve different condition-scoped values without choosing the largest. | Do not detach a voltage/current value from its conditions. |

## Exclude or resolve

Reject near-pitch or different pinning as drop-in; do not detach voltage from its stated conditions or infer approval from a housing badge.

## Questions to copy into a supplier enquiry

Does the drawing match the exact footprint? Is the mounting process supported? Which rating conditions and model-scoped documents apply?

## Evidence and price basis

BUYER_STATED is explicit buyer input; SOURCE_OBSERVED is a dated source; DERIVED is labeled interpretation. OBSERVED does not mean SUPPLIER_CONFIRMED. Missing facts remain UNKNOWN; TO_CONFIRM describes the next question. Preserve currency, unit, quantity tier, SKU and date; a listing is not a supplier-confirmed quote. Never infer stock, MOQ waivers or certification.
